# Recommend a catalog product

Recommend products for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Use the catalog MCP tools or resource; do not invent products, prices, or stock.
2. Ask one clarifying question when the use case or budget is missing.
3. Prefer in-stock products at or below the stated budget.
4. Give at most three options with a one-sentence reason for each.
5. State clearly when no catalog item satisfies every constraint.
